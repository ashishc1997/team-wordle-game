How to play Team Wordle

Two teams. One hidden five-letter word at a time. Each team has the clock to solve as many as they can — five points a word, and the highest score takes it.

1 The objective

A five-letter word is hidden. Your team has five guesses to find it. Every guess must be five letters, and every guess comes back colour-coded to tell you how close you were — so each attempt narrows down the answer.

Solve it within those five guesses and your team banks the points. Run out of guesses and the word is revealed, worth nothing, and the next word loads straight away. You cannot skip a word — if it's on the board, you play it out.

2 What the colours mean

After you submit a guess, every letter turns one of three colours. The colour is about that letter in that position.

A
Green — right letter, right place

This letter is in the word and exactly where you put it. Lock it in and build around it.

R
Yellow — right letter, wrong place

The word does contain this letter, but somewhere else. Keep it, move it.

C
Grey — not in the word

This letter isn't in the word at all. Rule it out and don't spend another guess on it.

3 A worked example

The hidden word is GRASS
C H A I R

Opening guess. A is in the right spot, R is in the word but belongs elsewhere, and C, H and I are out.

T R A C K

Moving R one place left turns it green. Two letters locked, three to find.

G R A S S

Solved on the third guess. +5 points, and the next word loads.

The one rule that trips people up

A letter only gets a colour for as many times as it actually appears in the hidden word. Guess SASSY against GRASS — which has two S's — and the three S's come back differently:

S A S S Y

One S is green because it landed in the right place. One is yellow — there's a second S elsewhere. The third is grey, even though the word contains S, because both S's are already accounted for.

4 The team rules

  • Scoring

    +5 for each word solved within five guesses. A missed word scores nothing.

  • The clock

    Each team gets the same amount of time — 10 minutes by default. It runs continuously across as many words as you can get through.

  • No passing

    A word you can't see still has to be played out. Use all five guesses or solve it.

  • Time's up

    The turn stops the moment the clock hits zero. A word in progress scores nothing, even on a promising board.

  • Fair draw

    Each team gets its own set of randomly drawn words. No overlap, so nothing is given away by watching the other team.

  • Guesses

    Any five letters is a valid guess. Probe words like CRANE or SLATE are fair game and are a good way to open.

  • Winning

    Highest score after both turns. Level scores are a tie — no tie-break.

5 Controls

A–ZType a letter
EnterSubmit the guess
BackspaceDelete a letter
EscPause and stop the clock

The on-screen keyboard works too, and it dims the letters you've already ruled out.
